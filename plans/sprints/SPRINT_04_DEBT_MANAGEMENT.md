# Sprint 04 — Debt Management

## Goal
Implement debt CRUD, search, filters, and debt detail.

## Tasks
- [ ] Debt entity and validation.
- [ ] Debt create/edit form.
- [ ] Debt list and detail screen.
- [ ] Customer and group selectors.
- [ ] Unassigned group support.
- [ ] Search by title/customer.
- [ ] Filters by group, due date, derived payment state.
- [ ] Sort options.
- [ ] Archive debt.
- [ ] Derive paid/outstanding/status from payments.
- [ ] Tests for calculations and validation.

## Acceptance criteria
- Debt can be created offline.
- Invalid amount/customer is rejected.
- Debt appears in correct group.
- Search/filter work offline.
- Derived status is consistent with payment total and due date.
